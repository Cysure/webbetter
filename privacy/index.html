Privacy policy.Clear, short and in line with Swiss and EU law.

This policy explains which personal data WB Solutions processes when you visit webbetter.ch, contact us or apply for a job, and which rights you have.

beratung@webbetter.ch

Last updated: 16 October 2026

1. Who is responsible

WB Solutions
Web Better is a brand of WB Solutions.
Email: beratung@webbetter.ch

WB Solutions is the controller for the processing of personal data on this website. For any privacy question, write to the address above.

2. Legal framework

We process personal data in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP, revised DSG) and, where it applies to visitors from the European Union and the EEA, including Germany and Austria, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Depending on the purpose, we rely on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), the performance or preparation of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) or our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3. Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA). When you visit it, technical data is processed automatically: IP address, date and time, the page requested, referrer, browser and operating system. We need this data to deliver the website securely and to detect misuse (legitimate interest). Log data is kept only for a short period. Data transferred to the USA is protected by the EU-U.S. and Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or by standard contractual clauses.

4. Contact and project enquiries

When you use the enquiry form or write to us by email, we process the details you provide, such as name, company, email, phone, budget and message, to answer your enquiry and prepare a proposal (contract preparation and legitimate interest). Form messages are delivered to us through the email service Resend (USA). We keep enquiries as long as needed to handle them and as required by statutory retention periods.

5. Job applications

When you apply through the careers form, we process your name, email, role, portfolio link, message and CV only to assess your application. Applications are deleted no later than six months after the application process ends, unless you agree that we keep them for future openings.

6. Cookies and local storage

We only use storage that is necessary for this website to work. It is kept in your browser's local storage, not in tracking cookies:

  • wb-consent: your privacy choice
  • webbetter-lang: your language
  • webbetter-theme: light or dark display

We currently use no analytics or marketing cookies. If we add them, they will only run after you have agreed in the consent banner. You can change your choice at any time under Cookie settings.

7. External services

To display this website consistently, fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (Google Ireland Ltd. and Google LLC) and a script library from the jsDelivr network. Your browser connects to these servers, which transmits your IP address. We use these services in our legitimate interest in a fast and uniform presentation. Images, logos and paintings are stored on our own server.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. To exercise your rights, write to beratung@webbetter.ch. You may also lodge a complaint with the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or, in the EU, with the supervisory authority of your country.

9. Security

This website is delivered exclusively over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS). We use technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss and unauthorised access.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The current version is always available on this page.
