[ GEO QUESTIONS ]

GEO, answered.

What is GEO (generative engine optimization)?

GEO is the practice of making a website easy for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot and Claude to read, trust and quote. It combines clear entities, citable facts, structured data and files like llms.txt, so your brand is cited inside AI answers.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO earns a ranking in a list of links. GEO earns a mention inside the answer itself. Both need a fast, well-structured site, but GEO adds a focus on facts, sources and entities that language models can verify and cite.

Can you guarantee that my brand appears in ChatGPT or Google?

No one can honestly guarantee placements, because each search engine and AI assistant decides for itself. What we guarantee is the work: a site that is fast, structured, factual and easy for every engine to read, trust and cite, plus transparent tracking of the results.

What is llms.txt?

llms.txt is a short plain-text file at the root of a website that summarises who you are, what you offer and which pages matter most, in a format language models can read quickly. We create and maintain it as part of GEO.

Which AI assistants do you optimise for?

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google Gemini and AI Overviews, Microsoft Copilot and Claude. They source answers differently, so we cover both the Google and Bing indexes and the AI crawlers themselves.

How can I check whether AI assistants mention my brand?

Ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot and Claude the questions your customers would ask, and note whether your brand is named or cited. We run this check systematically across many questions and track the results every month.

Should I allow or block AI crawlers?

If you want to be found and cited in AI answers, allow them. Blocking crawlers such as GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot or ClaudeBot can remove your content from those assistants. webbetter.ch allows them all in its robots.txt.

Does GEO replace SEO?

No. GEO builds on SEO. Most AI assistants retrieve pages from search indexes such as Google and Bing, so a fast, well-ranked, well-structured website remains the foundation for being cited.

How do Google AI Overviews choose their sources?

AI Overviews draw on pages from Google's index that answer the question clearly and are considered helpful and reliable. Strong SEO, clear answers near the top of the page and structured data all improve the chance of being included.
