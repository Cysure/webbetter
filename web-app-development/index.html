[ DEVELOPMENT QUESTIONS ]

Web and app development, answered.

Which technologies and CMS do you use?

We choose the stack for each project: modern frameworks such as Next.js or Astro, a headless CMS such as Sanity, Storyblok or WordPress, and Shopify for commerce. Your team can edit content without touching code.

Will my website work on mobile and be accessible?

Yes. Every site is designed mobile-first, tested on real devices and built to the WCAG accessibility guidelines, so it works for every visitor and every screen size.

Can you build an online shop?

Yes. We design and build e-commerce on Shopify or custom headless stacks, including product pages that rank, fast checkout and integrations with payments, inventory and marketing tools.

Will my website respect GDPR and Swiss data protection?

We build privacy-first: consent only where it is needed, privacy-friendly analytics and a setup aligned with the GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). Legal texts such as your privacy policy should be reviewed by your legal advisor.

How fast will my new website be?

We design and build for green Core Web Vitals on real devices: fast loading, no layout shift and quick responses to every tap. The live numbers in the footer of this page are measured in your browser right now.

Can I update the website content myself?

Yes. We set up an editor that fits your team, with reusable blocks, previews and roles, and we train your team at handover.

What are Core Web Vitals?

Core Web Vitals are Google's measurements of real user experience: Largest Contentful Paint (how fast the main content appears, good under 2.5 seconds), Interaction to Next Paint (how quickly the page responds, good under 200 milliseconds) and Cumulative Layout Shift (how stable the layout is, good under 0.1). They are part of how Google evaluates pages.

How do you make a website faster?

We render content on the server, optimise and lazy-load images, subset and preload fonts, remove unused JavaScript and CSS, cache at the edge on a global network and measure every release against Core Web Vitals.

Why does page speed matter for SEO and sales?

Fast pages rank better, keep visitors longer and convert more of them into customers. Speed also lowers advertising costs, because landing page experience affects ad quality scores.

What is conversion rate optimisation (CRO)?

CRO means improving the share of visitors who take action, such as sending an enquiry or buying. We use analytics, clear page structure, stronger calls to action and A/B tests to turn more of your existing traffic into results.

How do images affect website performance?

Images are usually the heaviest part of a page. We serve modern formats such as WebP and AVIF, size every image for the screen it appears on, lazy-load what is below the fold and always add descriptive alt text for accessibility and search.
